# Welcome to Fauna Social

An app that runs on all your devices and supports open social standards. Your data on a server you control.

## Tired of walled gardens?

You are not alone. Letting you leave would ruin their business model, whether or not you are paying. If you feel locked in, manipulated or surveilled, it is probably by design.

## Try Fauna Social

We integrate the open social networks to give you all the features you are used to, but without the lock-in or surveillance. [See what you get.](/what)

Our [bylaws](/organization/bylaws) ensure that the apps are free and open source.

## The cost

It does cost some money to store your backups and let people send you messages while you are on an airplane.

You pay this directly to a hosting provider for an always-on server, and to a domain registrar for your handle (`you@example.com`). We are not involved or informed.

Own your data and social experience for about the price of a coffee each month.

## Freedom to move

You can move anytime. Your identity is not an account anywhere or even your handle, but a cryptographic key only you can use. Your server can never read it, and neither can we.

Unhappy with your hosting provider? Switch, restore, carry on. The people who write to you will not notice.
