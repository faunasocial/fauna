# Deleting your account

*Illustration: turtle*

This page is about the Fauna Social app, made by {legalName}. It explains how
to delete your Fauna account and the data that goes with it.

## Where your account is

Your Fauna account is on a nest: the server program that you run yourself, or
that the admin of the nest you joined runs. {legalName} holds no account of
yours and no copy of your data, so the deletion happens on that nest. The one
exception is fauna.build, the server we keep so that app-store reviewers and our
own testers can try the apps. The accounts on it are ones we created, and we
delete them ourselves.

## Deleting it in the app

1. **Take your data first.** In **Settings → Account**, **Export My Data**
   downloads what your nest holds for you, your mail included. To move your
   mailbox to another mail service, use **Export mailbox**, among the mail
   pages in Settings.
2. **Delete.** In **Settings → Account**, choose **Delete Account** and confirm.
3. **Wait 14 days.** The deletion is scheduled, not immediate. For those 14 days
   your account works as before, and you can call the deletion off under
   **Pending actions** on the same page.

After the 14 days your nest deletes your account: your handle, your posts, your
messages and mail, your files and folders, your contacts, your calendar and your
settings. Where your posts went out to other networks, your nest asks those
networks to delete them too. Each network decides whether to honour that.

A few records stay on the nest. The software sets no end date for them; they
last as long as the nest's own records do:

- the nest's log of account and admin actions;
- records of moderation and of removals made under a legal obligation;
- records of paid subscriptions;
- the record of any change of your key.

What you sent to other people (messages, mail, posts they already received)
stays with them, as a letter does once it is delivered. Deleting the account
does not touch your devices: the app and your key stay on them until you remove
the app.

The app tells you if something must be settled before a deletion can go
ahead. You may need to hand over a group you own that someone else could take
over, or the supervised accounts you look after.

## If you run your own nest

On your own nest, you are its admin, and an admin's own account cannot be
deleted. To remove everything, use **Factory Reset** under the nest's **Danger
Zone** in the admin pages. It deletes every account and everything in them.
You can also shut the server down at your hosting provider.

## Without the app

Ask the admin of the nest that holds your account. An admin can remove an
account from their nest. You are warned for two weeks and keep full access, so
you can export everything; then access ends, and two weeks later the nest
deletes the account and its data, as above. We cannot do it for you, because we
do not run that nest.
