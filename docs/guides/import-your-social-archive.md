# Bring your posts from Facebook or Instagram

Your old posts, photos and events can live in Fauna too, at the dates you posted them. Unless you choose **Only me**, a public post can be seen on Fauna, a Friends-only post by the people who follow you, and everything else only by you.
Fauna never talks to Facebook or Instagram for this. You download the export archive the service gives you, and the Fauna app reads it on your own device.

## 1. Request your archive

- **Facebook:** Settings & privacy → Settings → Accounts Center → Your
  information and permissions → Download your information. Choose *Download
  to device*, the **JSON** format, and any media quality.
- **Instagram:** Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information. Choose *Download to device* and the **JSON** format. Instagram's archive does not record who could see each post, so your Instagram posts are imported visible only to you, and so is a post you had archived on Instagram.

The service emails you when the archive is ready. The download link expires
after a few days, so save the zip file somewhere you can find it.

## 2. Import it

Open **Settings → Import from other services** in Fauna and follow the six
steps:

1. **Source** — pick the service and read how to request the archive there;
   the step gives the address of the service's own download page.
2. **Archive** — choose the zip you downloaded (on the terminal app, type its
   path). Fauna reads the archive's table of contents and shows what it holds.
3. **What to import** — tick the categories you want. Posts, albums and events
   become your own Fauna content; comments, reactions, friends and message threads are kept inside the archive and are not published, except that once you link with a friend who imported too, your own comments and reactions on their posts come back as your own posts where Fauna can match them. The comments the archive holds under your own posts are shown beneath the post they belong to where Fauna can match them, and your message threads are listed in **Conversations**, marked with a box icon and read-only — both for you alone. This step also tells you how many posts carry a recorded audience: a public one can be seen on Fauna and a Friends-only one by the people who follow you, and every other post — whatever its audience there, or none recorded — is visible only to you. Choose
   **Only me** to keep everything private regardless. To import only part of
   the archive, fill in a from and/or to date (written like 2019-06-30): both
   days are included in full, counted in UTC.
4. **Confirm** — the totals, then **Start import**.
5. **Importing** — watch the progress for each kind of record, how long the
   import has been running and about how long is left; pause or cancel at any
   time. What has landed stays. A record the app cannot read is skipped and
   listed, and never stops the rest. You can even close the app: opening this page again picks the
   import up where it stopped.
6. **Done** — **View imported posts** opens your feed showing only the posts you brought in from that service, under a note that names it; **Show all posts** returns to your whole feed. Here you can also review anything that was skipped, put the profile name, bio and links from the archive on your Fauna profile in one step (from Instagram, its picture too; each one replaces yours, and the rest of your profile stays), and find the archive itself under **Settings → Folders**.

## What you get

- Each imported post shows a small archive badge and keeps its original date.
- A post that was *Friends only* is visible to the people who follow you. A post with any other audience there — Facebook's Only me, Friends of friends, Friends except… or a list of friends you picked — or whose audience the archive did not record — every Instagram post, since Instagram's archive records none — is visible only to you.
- Your events land in your calendar, visible only to you, at their original
  times and places and with your own reply: an event you went to reads
  *Going*, one you declined *Declined*, one you marked interested
  *Interested*. Events you hosted are yours as organizer; the other guests are
  kept inside the archive, not added to the event.
- The untouched archive is stored encrypted in a folder on your nest. Delete
  the folder whenever you like; the posts it produced are ordinary posts,
  deleted like any other.
- Importing the same archive again, or a newer one, adds only what is new.

## Link with a friend who imported too

A friend from your archive who is one of your contacts on Fauna, and has imported their own archive from the same service, can link with you. When either of you finishes an import while you are contacts, Fauna asks each of you once, on the other's row in **Contacts**, whether they are the person from your archive. The friend who did not just finish the import is asked first, and the other only once that friend has said yes. Answer **Yes, link** or **No**. A no is remembered: you are not asked again, and the two of you are not linked.

Once you have both said yes, your comments and reactions on each other's posts come back on Fauna where Fauna can match them to the post they were on (a comment's words, not its photos), each at its original date and signed by the person who wrote it:

- only on a post its writer can see on Fauna;
- today only on Facebook posts that shared a link, and no Instagram ones yet;
- a comment on a Friends-only post only when you each follow the other, and then it can be seen by the people who follow its writer.

Where your friend's comment already showed beneath your post for you alone, their own signed one takes its place. The contact's row then says you are linked ("Linked: Facebook", for a Facebook link), and adds how many could not be matched to a post when there are any.

**Unlink** on the same row stops anything more coming back. What already came back stays: it is its writer's own post, deleted like any other.

## Not yet

A comment under one of your posts that Fauna cannot match to it is kept in the archive but shown nowhere, and the Done step says how many there were ("3 comments could not be placed under a post"). Today Fauna can match only comments on Facebook posts that shared a link, and no Instagram comments yet.
