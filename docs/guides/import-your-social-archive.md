# Bring your posts from Facebook or Instagram

Your old posts, photos and events can live in Fauna too — at the dates you
posted them, visible to the same kind of people who could see them there.
Fauna never talks to Facebook or Instagram for this. You download the export
archive those services give you, and the Fauna app reads it on your own
device.

## 1. Request your archive

- **Facebook:** Settings & privacy → Settings → Accounts Center → Your
  information and permissions → Download your information. Choose *Download
  to device*, the **JSON** format, and any media quality.
- **Instagram:** Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and
  permissions → Download your information. Choose *Download to device* and
  the **JSON** format.

The service emails you when the archive is ready. The download link expires
after a few days, so save the zip file somewhere you can find it.

## 2. Import it

Open **Settings → Import from other services** in Fauna and follow the six
steps:

1. **Source** — pick the service and read how to request the archive there.
2. **Archive** — choose the zip you downloaded (on the terminal app, type its
   path). Fauna reads the archive's table of contents and shows what it holds.
3. **What to import** — tick the categories you want. Posts, albums and events
   become your own Fauna content; comments, reactions, friends and message
   threads are kept inside the archive for later features and never
   published. This step also tells you how many posts carry a recorded
   audience: those import to it, the rest are visible only to you. Choose
   **Only me** to keep everything private regardless. To import only part of
   the archive, fill in a from and/or to date (written like 2019-06-30): both
   days are included in full, counted in UTC.
4. **Confirm** — the totals, then **Start import**.
5. **Importing** — watch the progress, pause or cancel at any time. What has
   landed stays. You can even close the app: opening this page again picks the
   import up where it stopped.
6. **Done** — jump to your feed, review anything that was skipped, and find
   the archive itself under **Settings → Folders**.

## What you get

- Each imported post shows a small archive badge and keeps its original date.
- A post that was *Friends only* is visible to the people who follow you. A
  post that was *Only me*, or whose audience the archive did not record, is
  visible only to you.
- The untouched archive rests in a sealed folder on your nest. Delete the
  folder whenever you like; the posts it produced are ordinary posts, deleted
  like any other.
- Importing the same archive again, or a newer one, adds only what is new.

## Not yet

The profile name and bio from the archive are offered on the Done step but
cannot be applied yet, other people's comments on your imported posts are kept
but not shown, and message threads are kept but not listed. These arrive in
later releases.
