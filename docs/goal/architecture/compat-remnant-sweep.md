## Program 1 — the auth and trust remnants (2026-09-24)

Tranche 1 (2026-09-24) removed the auth/trust remnants: the NAT-mode V1 verify arm, its `nat_mode_v2` advert and the client's sign-V1 degrade (V2, nest-bound, is the only form — [`transport-connection.md`](transport-connection.md)); the untagged `CertBinding.sig` half and the verifier's fallback to it (`tagged_sig`, required, is the only signature — [`key-material-hierarchy.md`](key-material-hierarchy.md) § Architectural rules #8); the verify path's challenge-nonce-only retry for a pre-fold nest and its downgrade-harvest residual ([`security.md`](security.md) § Transport trust); the pre-Track-2 `SkippedLegacyNest` first-contact fallback (a kind-rejection hard-fails on every host); the `expires_in` optional-with-`expires_at`-fallback shape on every bearer mint (`expires_in` required — [`../behavior/login.md`](../behavior/login.md) § Token lifetime on the client's clock); the storage-mode transition contract's wire shims and the client's post-claim compat commit ([`nest/storage-modes.md`](nest/storage-modes.md) § The transition contract); and the dead-registered kinds of [`version-compatibility.md`](version-compatibility.md) § Dimension 2 → *The one exception* (a second user-population-ruling application). Each removal flipped its "old form still accepted" pin to a refusal. The retired `.v1` tags stay registered so their bytes are never reused — the registry's own rule, not a remnant.
