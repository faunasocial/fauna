# The fleet's utilization — public summary

> The full document describes part of the maintainers' private development
> setup and is not published. Other documents in `docs/goal/` that reference
> `fleet-utilization.md` resolve here.

## What a contributor needs to know

Nothing in this repository requires any of it. A plain `cargo build` of the
crate you change is the supported path. How the maintainers' own development
machines keep every working session seat in use — or say in one word why a
seat is idle — is private infrastructure and out of scope for this repository;
the scheduling it sits beside is summarized in
[`fleet-scheduler.md`](fleet-scheduler.md).
