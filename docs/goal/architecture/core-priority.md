## What a contributor needs to know

Nothing in this repository requires any of it. A plain `cargo build` of the
crate you change is the supported path. How the maintainers' development
machines share CPU among their own builds, and how one build is given a larger
share when it is urgent, is private infrastructure and out of scope for this
repository.
