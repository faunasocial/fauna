> **Audience:** the shared-Rust work in `libs/fauna-conversations` and `libs/fauna-client-conversations`, the tui build that leads, and the six apps' trickle-down.
> **Purpose:** how a member searches a community room's words from the app, and how a hit — a message's position, or a room post's id — becomes something the member can open.

## Goal

A community room is unbounded, and a member's device holds only the slice of the log it has drained, so the room's words are the one thing a member cannot search on their own device — the home nest searches them, through `fauna.conversations.room.search`, and answers *where*, never *what* ([`community-rooms.md`](community-rooms.md) § The three classes → *A derived view is only a purpose served once a member can reach it*, which owns the door and its rule). Until the federated follow lands, that same door is also the only way a member homed on another nest meets a room post whose author they do not follow: a post hit, with the room's recorded home, opens through the cross-nest deep-link relay ([`../architecture/feed-read-model.md`](../architecture/feed-read-model.md) § The read model → *Opening a post the timeline never loaded* → *A post homed on another nest*). This doc owns the surface; the behavior owners above own what the nest indexes, serves and withholds.

## The surface

**Where it lives — on the room's thread view, never on the Search page.** The door is per room (`room_id` is required) and a message hit is a position in that room's log, meaningful only inside the room; so the search sits on the `conversations` page's `conversation_detail` sub-page, not as an arm of [`../ui/search.md`](../ui/search.md), whose rows name posts, mail and drafts across the account. `room-search-button` sits on `thread-header` beside `thread-room-settings-button`, painted and live iff `capabilities.can_search_room` — a derived capability, true exactly for a community-class room whose floor seats this account (the door refuses every other class; an end-to-end room's words rest only on its members' devices and a client-side search over them is the content index's engine, [`content-index.md`](content-index.md), a separate surface this doc does not specify), so no app branches on class. Activating it opens the **search strip** between `thread-header` and the message stream: `room-search-field` and `room-search-submit-button`, the Search page's own shape — the richest of the two existing search shapes (the feed's live `feed-search-bar` re-queries as you type through `FeedManager::set_search_query`; `conversation-search-box` is a local list filter), priority #4. The strip is view state on the detail (`ThreadDetail.room.search`), never a draft: switching threads clears it, as every selection path clears the selected message.

**Submit-driven, no debounce** — [`../ui/search.md`](../ui/search.md) § Where logic lives → *Search debouncing*, the same ruling: Enter in the field and the submit button run one verb, `manager.room_search(thread_id, query)`; a blank query is dropped by the manager without a round-trip (the nest refuses it as `invalid_params`). The manager asks for one page of `ROOM_SEARCH_DEFAULT_LIMIT` hits (20 today; the nest clamps at `ROOM_SEARCH_MAX_LIMIT`), always with `include_posts` set, since this surface renders post hits (an older nest ignores the flag and answers messages only). There is deliberately no *more* affordance in the first build — a narrower query is the remedy, the Search page's own answer before its load-more existed; one would be a new id needing approval (the Search page's `search-load-more-button` is the shape to copy).

**While a submitted query stands, the result list takes the message stream's place; closing the strip restores the stream.** The feed's shape (a search re-query replaces the list) and the one that works on a terminal and a phone alike: the stream and a result list stacked would leave one of them a few lines tall. Esc, or `room-search-button` again, closes the strip and clears the query and the results (`manager.close_room_search(thread_id)`); so does opening a hit.

**What a hit row paints — `room-search-result-item[i]`, `kind` attribute `message | post` for drivers.** The nest sends positions, ids and ranks and no text, so every word on a row is this device's own, resolved in shared Rust, never by an app. Rows paint in the order served — best first, message and post hits interleaved on the one rank scale they come from — and are never re-sorted client-side ([`../ui/feed.md`](../ui/feed.md) § Anti-patterns). A **message** hit is the message the device holds at that log position (a device drains the whole channel it is keyed for, and the door serves only positions the caller's wraps open, so a hit is a message this device holds or is about to receive): sender, time and the first line of the opened text — the `conversation-list-item` snippet shape. A message hit whose position this device has not received yet paints a time-less row reading *not on this device yet* (shared copy, `conversations.unified.room_search_hit_pending`) and still opens: the selection it sets lights on the emit that fetches it (*The selected message*). A **post** hit paints the post's author, time and teaser, read from its envelope — the plaintext floor every follower of the author already sees ([`restricted-posts.md`](restricted-posts.md) § Encryption at rest) — through the post door: `fauna.posts.get`, or the relay where the room's recorded home is another nest; **never through the one deep-link slot**, which holds exactly one post, and never decoded by an app. The conversations manager takes that post read as a seam (the `RoomPostSeam` pattern turned around — the feed manager already takes the room's keys from this side), so the hit list is one snapshot and no app stitches two managers' answers together; the crate placement of the seam is the build's, under priority #2.

**Opening a hit — the row's typed `navigation` target, the Search page's `SearchNav` shape.** `RoomSearchNav::Message { thread_id, message_id }` → `manager.select_thread_and_message` (*The selected message* owns the rest: the strip closes, the stream returns with the message selected and brought into view — on tui the focus ring lands on it). `RoomSearchNav::Post { post_id, home_nest_url }` → app glue hands it to the feed manager exactly as a Search result's `Post` arm does: `FeedManager::resolve_post_from(post_id, home)` when `home_nest_url` is `Some` — the room's recorded home when it is another nest (`RoomPostKeys::room_home_nest_url`) — else `resolve_post`, then the feed's `post_detail`. A post hit on a room homed elsewhere is therefore the discovery door [`../ui/feed.md`](../ui/feed.md) names; the relay and its byte legs are that doc's.

**Mobile and tui.** On Android and iOS the strip and the results live on the detail screen with the same ids. tui declares no absence: the strip is a labelled field like `feed-search-field`, the results a focusable list, and the ring follows the selection after an open.

## Element IDs — approved under rule A 2026-10-19, the smallest set

Proposed by the design pass 2026-10-16 and approved by the user 2026-10-19 with one spelling change — the hit row is `room-search-result-item`, the twin of the Search page's `search-result-item`, not the proposed `room-search-result`. ui.yaml owns them (the `conversations` page); each with its one-line purpose:

- `room-search-button` — on `thread-header`; opens and closes the search strip; painted and live iff `capabilities.can_search_room`.
- `room-search-field` — the strip's one text input; Enter submits.
- `room-search-submit-button` — beside the field, the Search page's shape, for pointer and touch; the same verb as Enter.
- `room-search-result-item[i]` — one indexed row per hit, `kind` attribute `message | post`; activation opens the hit through its typed target.
- `room-search-no-results` — the empty state after a submitted query answered zero hits.

In ui.yaml: the five under `elements`; `room-search-button` in the `conversation_detail` sub-page's list and all five under the `conversations` page's `optional_elements` (the button is absent off a community room, the other four while the strip is closed). No new sub-page: the strip and its results paint inside `conversation_detail`. No new error id: `error-message` (convention 2). No *more* button: the door takes a clamped `limit` and no cursor, so there is nothing to page (the user's ruling, 2026-10-19).

## Where logic lives

**Shared Rust (`libs/fauna-conversations`, over `libs/fauna-client-conversations`):** `ConversationsManager::room_search(thread_id, query)` picks the leg by the channel's recorded home — `fauna.conversations.room.search` for a room this nest homes, the `fauna.conversations.room.search_remote` relay for one homed elsewhere, as every `_remote` relay is picked (`send` vs `send_remote`; the relay is [`../architecture/federation.md`](../architecture/federation.md)'s) — sets `include_posts`, asks for one default page, resolves every hit into a row's words from what this device can open (a message by its position; a post's author, time and teaser through the post-read seam) and into its typed `navigation`, and publishes the strip's state and rows as `ThreadDetail.room.search` (`{ query, status: Idle | Searching | Done | Failed, results }`, each row a `RoomSearchResultSnapshot` carrying its `kind`, words, `pending` flag and typed `navigation`); `open_room_search(thread_id)` opens the strip with nothing submitted (`Idle`) and `close_room_search(thread_id)` clears it; a reply that lands after the strip closed or another query was submitted is dropped. `capabilities.can_search_room` is derived where the room overlays its gating (`RoomSnapshot::gate`) from the room's class and this account's floor seat, and — like `encryption` — is never a rail's or a bridge's declaration. FFI and wasm faces additive.

**App glue:** paints the strip and the rows from the snapshot, runs the two verbs, navigates on the typed target (a `Post` target through the feed manager's deep-link door), and nothing else — it never names a kind, never decodes a post, never re-sorts, never branches on class.

**The page's § User actions rows** for the five ids live in [`../ui/conversations.md`](../ui/conversations.md) § User actions, as every element of that page's does.

## Errors & edge cases

- **Empty.** `room-search-no-results` is painted after a submitted query answered zero hits. An empty list is a real answer that covers three states a member cannot tell apart, deliberately: nothing matched, the room was never keyed to its home nest, and the members rotated the nest's read out ([`community-rooms.md`](community-rooms.md)'s rule) — so the copy says only *"Nothing in this room matches."* (`conversations.unified.room_search_no_results`).
- **Refused or failed.** Not a floor member (`permission_denied`), the relay to the room's home failing, the nest unreachable — the message surfaces on `error-message` (convention 2) and the result region stays blank; a failed search never paints the empty state.
- **In flight.** Between submit and the reply the result region is blank and no element says *searching*: an e2e waits on a result row, the empty state or the error, never on a timer (convention 14).
- **A hit this device cannot open** is not expected — the door filters hits by the caller's wrap coverage — but a position the device has not drained yet is: its row reads *not on this device yet* and its selection lights when the message lands (§ The surface).

## Alternatives not taken

- **A nest-backed search over *every* thread's bodies** — the `fauna.conversations.search` kind [`../ui/conversations.md`](../ui/conversations.md) § Where logic lives used to name as a future feature. A community room's search is the room's own read by the class rule; a cross-room "search all my rooms" would be a Search page arm, not this surface, and no such kind exists.
- **The Search page as the venue** — a hit is a position in one room's log, meaningful only inside the room, and the door takes a `room_id`.
- **A results panel beside the stream** — no room for it on a terminal or a phone; the list replaces the stream instead.
- **A *more* button** — ruled out by the user 2026-10-19: the door takes a clamped `limit` and no cursor, so there is nothing to page; a narrower query is the remedy.
- **Snippets from the nest** — forbidden by the behavior owner; every word on a row is the device's own.
